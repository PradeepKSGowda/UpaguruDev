## Objective
Integrate Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and PostHog product analytics into Next.js 15 App Router. Implement non-blocking script loading via `next/script`, dynamic SPA route change tracking in `AnalyticsProvider.tsx`, client initialization modules (`lib/analytics/gtag.ts`, `lib/analytics/posthog.ts`), a type-safe `useAnalytics` hook (`hooks/useAnalytics.ts`) for custom candidate interaction telemetry (`search_performed`, `notification_viewed`, `filter_applied`, `outbound_click`), environment variable templates in `.env.example`, and an audit schema in `analytics-events-schema-v1.sql`.
