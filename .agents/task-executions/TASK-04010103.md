## Objective
Implement production-grade crawler modules for India's premier central government recruitment boards: Union Public Service Commission (`UPSCCrawler` targeting upsc.gov.in), Staff Selection Commission (`SSCCrawler` targeting ssc.gov.in), and Railway Recruitment Boards (`RRBCrawler` with multi-regional support). Each crawler operates through declarative YAML configurations, canonicalizes relative links, executes streaming PDF downloads with SHA-256 hash deduplication, extracts structured text via `pypdf`/`pdfplumber`, and records execution telemetry in Supabase `crawl_runs` and `pdf_documents`.
